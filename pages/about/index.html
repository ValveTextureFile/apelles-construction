Meet Nick.

At the heart of Apelles Construction is a commitment to craftsmanship, integrity, and customer satisfaction—values that have guided co-owner Nick Giannikouris throughout his career.

Nick holds a Bachelor of Architecture degree from the New York Institute of Technology and brings more than 30 years of hands-on construction and home renovation experience to every project. His extensive background spans residential construction, interior and exterior renovations, kitchens, bathrooms, flooring installation, masonry, and complete home transformations.

In addition to his construction expertise, Nick has accumulated over 25 years of project management experience, overseeing projects from initial planning and design through final completion. His architectural education provides a unique advantage, allowing him to blend functional design, structural understanding, and practical construction knowledge into every project.

Throughout his career, Nick has built a reputation for attention to detail, quality workmanship, clear communication, and delivering projects on time and within budget. Whether managing a complete home renovation, custom deck installation, kitchen remodel, or exterior improvement project, he approaches each job with the same level of professionalism and dedication.

As co-owner of Apelles Construction, Nick works closely with homeowners to transform their vision into reality, ensuring every project reflects the highest standards of craftsmanship, reliability, and customer service.

"Our goal is simple: build it right, build it to last, and treat every home as if it were our own."
